# ParkOrbit — Peer-to-Peer Parking Rental Platform (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview
ParkOrbit is a modern peer-to-peer parking rental marketplace connecting property owners, apartment residents, and commercial businesses with drivers searching for convenient, verified nearby parking. The visual identity strictly follows `DESIGN-mastercard.md`.

## 2. Core User Roles
- **Drivers**: Search by destination/landmark/geolocation, filter by time window/vehicle/price/type, inspect dimensions and rules, reserve parking with transparent pricing, receive a Booking Reference & Check-in PIN, cancel per policy, and review completed stays.
- **Parking Hosts**: Create and manage parking space listings (including saving incomplete drafts), set hourly/daily pricing, define dimensions & vehicle compatibility, manage opening hours & blocked time slots, verify driver check-in PINs, and track occupancy & pending/estimated earnings.
- **Unified Account**: Every user can seamlessly act as a driver, a host, or both under one account.
- **Trust & Admin Moderator**: Review reported listings/experiences, pause suspicious spaces, and configure platform commission parameters.

## 3. Design System (`DESIGN-mastercard.md`)
- **Canvas Cream (`#F3F0EE`)**: Default body background (never sterile white).
- **Lifted Cream (`#FCFBFA`)**: Elevated container surfaces.
- **Ink Black (`#141413`)**: Primary 20px-radius pill CTAs (`#F3F0EE` text), headings, and dark warm-black footer.
- **Signal Orange (`#CF4500`)** & **Light Signal Orange (`#F37338`)**: Eyebrow accent dots, compliance pills, and decorative curved orbital SVG paths.
- **Circular Portrait Cards**: Perfect circle image masks (`50%` radius) with attached `56px` white circular Satellite micro-CTAs (`→`) protruding ~40% at bottom-right.
- **Typography**: `Sofia Sans` / `MarkForMC` geometric sans with `-2%` headline tracking, `450` body weight, and `700` uppercase `+4%` eyebrow labels.
