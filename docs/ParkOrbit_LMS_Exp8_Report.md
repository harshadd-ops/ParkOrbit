## Phase 1: Empathize

### 1. Goal

To understand the real-life experiences of daily urban commuters, electric vehicle (EV) drivers, and residential or commercial property owners when searching for or sharing parking spaces across high-density areas in Pune, and to identify the physical, informational, trust, and navigational barriers they encounter.

### 2. Objectives

1. **Understand user needs:** Learn about the daily parking and charging requirements of two-wheeler and four-wheeler commuters, EV owners, and homeowners or housing societies with idle parking bays.
2. **Identify pain points:** Discover barriers such as on-street cruising congestion, lack of real-time slot availability, unclear height and dimensional clearances, cash haggling, and security concerns around letting strangers into private gates.
3. **Study existing practices:** Understand how drivers currently hunt for curbside parking in busy corridors (such as Koregaon Park, Baner, FC Road, and Hinjawadi) and how property owners manage vacant driveways or stilt bays.
4. **Understand user expectations:** Identify what verified information drivers need before travelling to a destination (hourly pricing, covered status, EV charger wattage, gate access instructions) and what controls hosts need to list their spots safely.
5. **Gather user insights:** Collect field observations and user feedback to guide the human-centred design of the ParkOrbit solution.

### 3. Target Users

1. Daily urban commuters and vehicle drivers (hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, and two-wheelers)
2. Electric Vehicle (EV) drivers seeking guaranteed destination charging and covered bays
3. Homeowners, gated villa residents, and commercial property managers (Hosts) with underutilized parking spaces
4. Community and platform Trust & Safety moderators managing verification and access disputes

### 4. Activities to Achieve the Objectives

| Activity | What we will do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **User interviews** | Ask daily commuters, EV owners, and Pune property owners about their experiences finding parking and managing vacant driveways. |
| **Observation** | Observe peak-hour curb cruising, parking signage, gate entry bottlenecks, and daytime empty residential bays across busy Pune hubs. |
| **Empathy mapping** | Organize findings into what drivers and property hosts say, think, do, and feel during daily parking and hosting scenarios. |
| **Pain-point identification** | Identify recurring parking search, vehicle dimension, pricing, and gate security challenges from the collected evidence. |
| **Requirement gathering** | Determine what geospatial, pricing, and access verification information users need to reserve and host parking spaces confidently. |

### 5. Interview Questions

1. What difficulties do you face when trying to find parking in busy Pune localities like Koregaon Park, Baner, FC Road, or Hinjawadi?
2. How do you currently check whether safe parking or an EV charger is available before driving to an unfamiliar area?
3. Have you encountered blocked driveways, low-clearance basements unsuitable for SUVs, unverified parking rates, or cash-only attendants?
4. What specific information (such as live hourly rates, bay dimensions, CCTV coverage, or gate entry instructions) would help you plan and book your parking before leaving home?
5. If you own an empty residential driveway or society bay during office hours, what security or management concerns prevent you from renting it out?
6. What would make checking into a private parking spot and reporting access or overstay problems easier and more trustworthy?

### 6. Expected Outcome

- A better understanding of both drivers' parking needs and hosts' security expectations.
- A structured record of commuter and homeowner interview responses and field observations.
- An empathy map representing driver frustration while circling blocks and host hesitation around gate security.
- A prioritized list of the most significant urban parking discovery, booking, and trust barriers.
- Initial insights to help clearly define the core problem in Phase 2.

## Phase 2: Define

### 1. Goal

To clearly define the main urban parking discovery and idle space utilization problem faced by commuters and property owners in high-density neighbourhoods, based on user needs and evidence gathered during the Empathize phase.

### 2. Objectives

1. **Analyze user research:** Review interview responses and field observations from Phase 1 to identify recurring difficulties faced by drivers and hosts.
2. **Identify the primary pain point:** Determine which informational and trust barrier creates the greatest friction in connecting searching drivers with empty private parking bays.
3. **Identify user needs:** Establish what real-time availability, pricing, vehicle compatibility, and gate security features users need to book and host spaces independently.
4. **Formulate a problem statement:** Express the identified problem clearly, specifying the target users, their needs, and the underlying challenge.
5. **Develop a How Might We question:** Frame the problem as an open-ended question that guides creative solutions in Phase 3, Ideate.

### 3. Identify the core problem

Potential problems to investigate include:

- Lack of reliable, real-time geospatial information about available parking bays and hourly prices near busy destinations.
- High daytime idle capacity (45–60%) in private residential driveways, bungalows, and society stilt spaces while drivers circle blocks outside.
- Uncertainty about bay dimensions, roof coverage, SUV height clearance, and working EV charging points.
- Security concerns and gate friction when sharing access codes or handling cash with unfamiliar drivers.
- Absence of a structured reporting and moderation mechanism to resolve blocked bays, inaccurate listings, or overstays.

### 4. Define the problem statement

Our proposed problem statement is:

> **Urban drivers and EV commuters in dense Pune corridors find it difficult to locate safe, predictable, and affordable parking because reliable, real-time information about available spaces, vehicle clearances, and EV chargers is not readily accessible—even though thousands of private driveways and society bays sit empty nearby. Both drivers and property owners need a trusted, accessible way to discover, book, verify gate access, and manage private parking spaces by the hour.**

### 5. Develop the “How Might We” question

How might we help urban commuters find reliable, real-time information about available private parking spaces and enable homeowners to share their idle driveways securely so that drivers can park with greater confidence and hosts can earn passive income effortlessly?

## Phase 3: Ideate

### 1. Goal

To generate, explore, and evaluate innovative, practical solutions that help urban commuters find reliable parking and EV charging spaces while enabling property owners to safely list and manage their idle parking bays.

### 2. Objectives

1. **Brainstorm solutions:** Generate multiple ideas to address the defined urban parking and space-sharing problem without judging them prematurely.
2. **Explore alternatives:** Consider physical hardware, community-based directories, and digital marketplace solutions.
3. **Encourage creativity:** Combine and improve ideas to identify frictionless, high-trust approaches.
4. **Evaluate feasibility:** Compare solutions based on usability, cost, accessibility, and potential urban and social impact.
5. **Select the best idea:** Choose a promising solution for development in Phase 4, Prototype.

### 3. Brainstorm Possible Solutions

**Idea 1: Live Parking Availability Map**  
An interactive digital map showing available parking spots, hourly rates, covered status, and EV charging points across Pune neighbourhoods.

**Idea 2: Community Slot Noticeboard & Barrier Reporting Directory**  
A neighbourhood directory and reporting interface where residents post vacant slot timings and commuters flag blocked entrances or inaccurate signage.

**Idea 3: Smart IoT Driveway Bollard & RFID Gate Hardware**  
Physical automated parking locks and RFID scanners installed in private driveways that unlock when a registered commuter vehicle arrives.

**Idea 4: ParkOrbit — Peer-to-Peer Map, Instant Booking & PIN-Verified Parking Application**  
A unified web and mobile-ready application that connects drivers with verified private hosts. It combines an interactive live map with `₹/hr` price pins, instant hourly booking with slot collision checks, a privacy-preserving 4-digit Gate PIN digital pass, a Host Workspace with interactive pin-drop space onboarding and earnings analytics, and an Admin Trust & Safety moderation console.

### 4. Comparing the Ideas

| Idea | Usability | Cost | Social impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Live Parking Availability Map | High potential | Low–medium | Medium–high |
| Community Slot & Reporting Directory | Medium | Low | Medium |
| Smart IoT Driveway Bollard Hardware | Medium | High | Medium–high |
| **ParkOrbit — Combined P2P Map, Booking & Gate PIN Application** | **High potential** | **Low–medium** | **High** |

### 5. Select the Best Solution

After evaluating the possible solutions based on usability, affordability, accessibility, and potential urban impact, we selected **ParkOrbit — Peer-to-Peer Map, Booking & Gate PIN Application** as the best solution.

**Selected Solution: ParkOrbit Web & Mobile Application**  
ParkOrbit is a peer-to-peer urban parking marketplace application designed to help commuters find and reserve verified private parking spots by the hour and empower homeowners to monetize empty bays with secure PIN check-ins and trust moderation.

**Key Features**

1. **Geospatial Discovery Map:** Displays available private parking spaces across Pune neighbourhoods (Koregaon Park, Baner, Hinjawadi, Kothrud, Viman Nagar, FC Road, Wakad, Magarpatta) with live `₹/hr` price pins and filters for vehicle type, space type, and budget.
2. **Space Specifications & Instant Booking:** Shows verified host badges, exact bay dimensions (length, width, height clearance), amenities (24/7 CCTV, 7.4kW EV charger, covered roof), and transparent fee calculation with automatic time-slot collision detection.
3. **Digital Parking Pass & Gate PIN Check-In:** Issues a one-time 4-digit Gate PIN (e.g., `4 8 2 9`) and private gate access instructions only after booking confirmation, protecting host privacy and eliminating cash haggling.
4. **Host Command Center & Pin-Drop Onboarding:** Enables homeowners to list a spot in under three minutes by dragging a pin on the map, configuring hourly rates, and tracking gross bookings, 12% platform commission, and net payouts.
5. **Trust, Safety & Barrier Reporting Moderation:** Allows users to report access issues or obscured signage and gives administrators a one-click console to suspend listings, resolve disputes, or adjust marketplace fee parameters.
6. **1-Click Multi-Persona Simulator:** Allows instant switching between Driver (Aarav), Host (Neha), and Admin (Nandini) perspectives for seamless evaluation and end-to-end testing.

**Why We Selected This Solution**

- **Usability:** Unifies live map discovery, hourly booking, digital Gate PIN verification, host space management, and admin moderation in one intuitive platform.
- **Affordability:** Operates entirely via software and 4-digit PIN verification without requiring expensive IoT bollards or municipal parking construction.
- **Accessibility:** Helps commuters and EV drivers lock in guaranteed, dimension-matched parking before starting their trip.
- **Social Impact:** Reduces on-street cruising congestion, cuts tailpipe carbon emissions, accelerates EV adoption, and creates passive income for local households.
- **Practicality:** Built as a full-stack Next.js 15, React 19, and Leaflet web application that works immediately across desktop and mobile devices.

## Phase 4 : Prototype

### 1. Goal

To create a functional prototype and visual representation of the selected solution, **ParkOrbit — Peer-to-Peer Urban Parking Marketplace**, to demonstrate how the application helps drivers discover and book private parking spots, verify access via Gate PINs, and how hosts and admins manage spaces and resolve access reports.

### 2. Objectives

- Develop interactive screens and high-resolution visual views of the main workflows of the ParkOrbit application.
- Demonstrate how drivers can search across Pune neighbourhoods, filter by vehicle and space type, and inspect live `₹/hr` price pins on an interactive map.
- Show how users can view dimensional clearances, EV charging capabilities, verified host credentials, and transparent fee breakdowns before booking.
- Illustrate how drivers receive a Digital Parking Pass with a 4-digit Gate PIN and step-by-step entry instructions in their trips hub.
- Demonstrate how hosts onboard new parking spaces via an interactive pin-drop map and monitor their earnings, payouts, and scheduled arrivals.
- Present the Admin Trust & Safety moderation interface for reviewing reported access barriers, suspending listings, and managing platform fees.

### 3. Prototype Development

A functional full-stack prototype of the ParkOrbit application was designed and developed using Next.js 15, React 19, Tailwind CSS, and Leaflet with Carto Voyager open-source map tiles. The interface follows a cohesive editorial design system using warm alabaster canvas (`#F6F2EA`), deep ink (`#141413`), and signal orange (`#F37338`), with clear iconography, high-contrast typography, and role-filtered navigation to support an effortless user experience across Driver, Host, and Admin personas.

The prototype consists of four core functional views:

1. **Live Geospatial Discovery Map:** Enables drivers to filter by Pune neighbourhoods, vehicle size, and amenities while comparing hourly rates directly on an interactive Leaflet map.
2. **Space Specifications & Booking Calculator:** Displays verified host credentials, bay clearance dimensions, and real-time hourly fee breakdowns (base rent + 5% service fee) with slot collision prevention.
3. **Driver Trips & Digital Gate PIN Pass:** Presents active reservations alongside a dark-themed pass showing the 4-digit Gate PIN (`4 8 2 9`), booking reference, and gate entry instructions.
4. **Host Workspace & Admin Governance:** Provides hosts with a pin-drop space onboarding wizard and 30-day revenue analytics, paired with an Admin Trust & Safety view for resolving reported access issues.

The prototype communicates the application's layout, navigation, and core features. It demonstrates how the solution makes private parking availability more transparent, supports better commute planning, and encourages community participation in sharing underutilized urban infrastructure.

## Conclusion

The Design Thinking process helped us systematically explore a critical urban mobility and space-utilization problem faced by daily commuters and property owners in Pune's high-density corridors. Through the Empathize, Define, Ideate, and Prototype phases, we developed **ParkOrbit — Peer-to-Peer Urban Parking Marketplace & Geospatial Mobility Platform**.

The application helps drivers discover available private parking spaces and EV chargers on an interactive map, verify vehicle clearance and pricing upfront, check in seamlessly using a 4-digit Gate PIN digital pass, and report access barriers. Simultaneously, it enables homeowners and housing societies to list empty driveways via an intuitive pin-drop wizard and track their earnings transparently, while administrators maintain marketplace trust through a dedicated moderation console.

This activity strengthened our understanding of user-centred problem-solving, multi-stakeholder empathy, structured idea evaluation, and full-stack prototyping. Overall, Project ParkOrbit demonstrates how design thinking and geospatial technology can transform dormant private infrastructure into accessible, sustainable, and community-driven urban mobility solutions.
